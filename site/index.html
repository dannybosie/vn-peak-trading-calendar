VN Peak Trading Calendar

Campaign Seasonality Intelligence for Vietnam e-commerce

Every marketplace sale day, statutory holiday and gifting date that moves demand in Vietnam, in one calendar you can subscribe to, with a prep reminder before each tentpole. Lunar dates are computed for the Vietnamese calendar, not copied.

    How the dates are made

    Marketplace days follow the double-day pattern (1.1 to 12.12) with mid-month and payday windows. Holidays follow the Labor Code 2019, Article 112, plus Vietnamese Culture Day (24/11) from 2026 under Resolution 28/2026/QH16. Officially announced day-off schedules are added to the event notes.

    Lunar dates (Tết, Vía Thần Tài, Giỗ Tổ, Vu Lan, Mid-Autumn, Ông Công Ông Táo) are computed for UTC+7 with the full new-moon series from Meeus's Astronomical Algorithms, and tested against published Vietnamese calendars for every festival from 2025 to 2028. The common shortcut algorithm puts Vu Lan 2026 on 26 August; the new moon actually fell 37 minutes after midnight, so it is 27 August.

    Prep reminders sit 60 days before Tết, 30 before Mid-Autumn, 21 before 11.11 and 12.12, and 7 to 14 days before the other weighted dates.
